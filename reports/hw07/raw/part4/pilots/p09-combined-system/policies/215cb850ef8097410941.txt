30-Day Membership
The 30-Day Membership offers a flexible way to ride with Capital Bikeshare when you need it.
$25 / 30-Day Membership includes:
Unlimited 45-minute rides on classic bikes for 30 days from purchase. Members pay $0.15 per minute for ebikes and avoid unlock fees.
24/7 access to thousands of bikes in Washington, DC; Arlington, VA; Alexandria, VA; Montgomery County, MD; Prince George's County, MD; Fairfax County, VA; City of Fairfax; and the City of Falls Church, VA.
If you need more time than what’s covered, pay $0.05 per minute. All per minute ride prices are rounded up to the nearest minute.
Please note that your ride will incur standard sales taxes according to the start location of your ride.
