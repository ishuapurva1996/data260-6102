Single Trip
The Single Trip is perfect for quick, one-way trips from point A to point B. No commitment necessary.
$1 Unlock + $0.15/min
The Single Trip costs $1 to unlock and $0.15/min on a classic bike or $0.35/min on an ebike.
Buy a pass through the app
Interested in Ebikes?
With a pedal-assist motor to supercharge your ride, our ebikes are the easiest way to get around the metro DC area. Pricing starts at $1 to unlock plus $0.35/minute.
Ebike parking is free at Capital Bikeshare stations. You’ll be charged $3 for parking an ebike at a public bike rack in the service area.
A $25 fee will be applied to any ebike that is not docked at a station or secured to a public bike rack with its cable lock, if it remains inactive for over 30 minutes after the ride concludes. This fee is in addition to the standard per-minute rate.
How to Buy a Single Trip
Single Trips can be purchased in the Capital Bikeshare app, Lyft app, or at any station kiosk.
Please note ebike rides can only be purchased via the Capital Bikeshare app or Lyft app.
